Wahoo Cribbage Privacy Policy

Wahoo Cribbage · Effective November 9, 2026

This policy covers the Wahoo Cribbage app for iPhone and iPad (“Wahoo”, “the app”), made by Benjamin Berard (“the developer”). It’s the same as the policy in the app, under Settings › Help › Privacy Policy.

The short version: Wahoo Cribbage has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no Wahoo account. The one piece of personal information Wahoo itself stores is your Game Center name, and only while an online game you set up for anyone to join is listed (described below). Playing with other people also sends your name and moves to them, through Apple's Game Center or directly to devices nearby; Apple handles Game Center, iCloud and purchases under its own policy.

On Your Device

Your settings, stats, saved games, the name you type for yourself, your saved theme mixes and your themes stay on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Online Games You Set Up

When you choose Set Up Game online, Wahoo lists the game in its public iCloud database so other Wahoo players can find it under Join a Game. The listing holds your Game Center name, the game's choices (live or turn-based, 61 or 121, how many players, partners or not), a matchmaking number and when it was listed.

Anyone browsing Join a Game can see it. The listing is deleted when the seats fill, the game starts or you cancel. A live game's listing expires two minutes after Wahoo last checked in, and a turn-based game's after three days. An expired listing is hidden from everyone at once; if it's still stored (the app was closed while you waited, or you were offline), Wahoo deletes it the next time you open online play on any device signed in to the same iCloud account. Until then it stays in the database, hidden; to have it removed sooner, email the address below.

Inviting friends through Game Center lists nothing. The developer can see listings in Apple's iCloud dashboard, uses them only to run the lobby, and doesn't share them.

Game Center

Online play uses Game Center. The players in a game see each other's Game Center names. A turn-based game's moves and the players' names are kept by Game Center until the game is removed. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy.

Nearby Play

Nearby play connects to devices in the same room over Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, with your permission for the local network. People nearby with Wahoo open can see the name you play under. Moves go straight between the devices.

Purchases and Tips

Themes and tips are bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; the developer never sees your payment details or who you are. A tip's thank-you (the Gold theme) is remembered in your own iCloud account so it follows you to your other devices.

Deleting Your Data

Cancel a game you set up to delete its listing at once, or email the address below to have any listing of yours deleted. Delete the app to delete everything on the device. Game Center and iCloud data are managed in Settings on your device and at privacy.apple.com.

Children

Wahoo asks no one for personal information. A child who sets up an online game shares their Game Center name the same way as anyone else, under the Game Center settings and restrictions a parent chooses in Screen Time. Purchases follow the parental controls of Family Sharing and Screen Time.

Changes and Questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and at tictacular.com/wahoo-privacy, with a new date. Questions, or a listing to delete: support@tictacular.com.

Apple’s privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy
