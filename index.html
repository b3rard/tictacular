13 ways to play

Every one of them free.

Start with the game you grew up with, then try a board that falls, a mark in two places at once, or nine games inside one.

Classic

3×3, three in a row.

Tic-Tac-Attack

Conquest with dice, for 2 to 5 players.

Toe-Tac-Tic

Classic backwards: three in a row loses.

Infinite

Three marks each, and your oldest vanishes.

Ultimate

Nine boards in one. Your move picks your opponent’s board.

Tapatan

Three pieces each, placed and then slid.

Big Boards

4×4, 5×5, 9×9 and 15×15 Gomoku.

Gravity

Drop marks into a 7×6 grid for four in a row, or play Tumble.

Stacker

Three sizes of piece. Big ones swallow small ones.

Numbers

Odds against evens. The first line to add up to 15 wins.

Order & Chaos

6×6. One side wants five in a row, the other stops it.

Quantum

Marks in two squares at once, until a loop collapses them.

Custom

Choose the rows, the columns and how many in a row wins.

Where it started

Whenever my dad and I went out to eat, the game started before the food did. One of us would borrow a pen, flatten a paper napkin on the table and draw the grid. X, O, X. Best of three, then best of five, then just one more, until the plates came out.

The napkins never made it home, but the games stayed with me. Tic-Tacular is for every one of those games.
